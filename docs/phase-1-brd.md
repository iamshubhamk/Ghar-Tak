# Phase 1 - Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Product: GharTak  
Initial market: Patna, Bihar, India  
Stage: Bootstrapped MVP  
Budget constraint: INR 0 during MVP  
Primary objective: validate demand with the first 100 users and first 50 completed bookings.

## 1. Problem Statement

Patna has a large base of households, renters, students, small offices, and local businesses that regularly need home and local services. Today, customers usually depend on word of mouth, local shops, WhatsApp contacts, Google searches, or unverified directory listings.

This creates trust and reliability problems:

- Customers do not know which provider is reliable.
- Pricing is unclear and inconsistent.
- Providers may not arrive on time.
- Service quality is difficult to judge before booking.
- Small providers lack a simple digital channel for new work.
- Disputes are handled informally with little accountability.
- Existing large platforms may not serve Patna at neighborhood depth.

GharTak will solve this by creating a verified hyperlocal service marketplace for Patna, connecting customers with trusted providers and helping providers receive structured local bookings.

## 2. Vision Statement

To become Patna's most trusted hyperlocal platform for finding, booking, and managing verified local services at home.

## 3. Mission Statement

GharTak helps Patna residents access reliable local services quickly, safely, and transparently while enabling local service providers to grow their income through digital bookings, reputation, and fair marketplace participation.

## 6. Success Metrics

North star metric: completed trusted bookings per week.

A trusted booking means:

- Customer created a booking.
- Provider accepted or admin assigned the provider.
- Service was completed.
- No serious unresolved complaint was raised.

### MVP Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Registered customers | 100 |
| Verified providers | 25-50 |
| Active categories | 5-7 |
| Completed bookings | 50 |
| Booking completion rate | 60%+ initially, improving to 75%+ |
| Average provider response time | Under 4 hours initially |
| Customer rating average | 4.0+ out of 5 |
| Repeat booking rate | 15%+ |
| Provider activation rate | 50%+ providers complete at least one booking |
| Infrastructure cost | INR 0 during MVP |

## 7. Competitor Analysis

| Competitor | Type | Strengths | Gaps | GharTak Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Urban Company | Managed home services marketplace | Strong brand, standardized services, mature UX | May not serve Patna neighborhood depth; operationally expensive | Patna-first local trust and lean operations |
| Justdial | Local search/directory | Large listings and SEO | Discovery-heavy; inconsistent verification and booking lifecycle | Verified providers plus structured booking |
| Sulekha | Service lead marketplace | Broad service coverage | Lead quality can vary; less controlled experience | Simpler booking with local trust |
| Google Search/Maps | Discovery | Free and familiar | No guaranteed verification or lifecycle | Curated local provider network |
| WhatsApp/word of mouth | Informal network | High trust when referrals exist | Not scalable; no history or reviews | Turn local trust into a structured product |
| Local shops/contractors | Offline access | Familiar and accessible | Limited digital reach | Bring local providers online simply |

Positioning:

> Patna's verified local service marketplace for reliable home services, built for local trust before scale.

## 8. Revenue Model

MVP should prioritize liquidity, not early monetization.

| Revenue Stream | Classification |
|---|---|
| Commission per completed booking | MVP, but can start at 0% |
| Featured provider listing | Post-MVP |
| Provider subscription | Post-MVP |
| Lead fee | Nice to Have, risky early |
| Customer convenience fee | Post-MVP |
| Online payment margin | Post-MVP |
| B2B maintenance contracts | Nice to Have |

Recommended MVP pricing:

- First 50 bookings: INR 0 provider fee.
- Bookings 51-150: test INR 20-50 fixed success fee.
- Later: category-specific 5%-10% for higher-value jobs.

## 9. Risks and Assumptions

### Assumptions

| Assumption | Confidence | Validation Method |
|---|---|---|
| Patna customers want verified providers | Medium | Landing page, manual bookings, WhatsApp outreach |
| Providers will join if leads are real | High | Direct onboarding and early bookings |
| Cash on Service is acceptable | High | Common local behavior |
| Web app is enough for MVP | Medium | Track mobile browser usage |
| Founder-led operations can handle first 100 users | High | Keep categories and localities narrow |

### Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Low provider reliability | High | Manual verification, ratings, disabling |
| Poor customer trust | High | Verified badges, reviews, admin support |
| Marketplace cold start | High | Start with few high-demand categories/localities |
| Too many categories too early | High | Begin with essential home services |
| Provider bypasses platform | High | Reputation, repeat convenience, simple policies |
| Operational overload | High | Simple workflows and founder-led learning |
| Paid infrastructure creep | Medium | Local/free-first architecture |

## 11. Strategic Recommendation

Launch as a simple, web-first, founder-operated marketplace.

Optimize for:

- Trust
- Verified supply
- Booking completion
- Customer feedback
- Provider onboarding
- Zero infrastructure cost

Avoid during MVP:

- Real-time chat
- Online payments
- Native mobile apps
- Complex scheduling
- AI recommendations
- Multi-city support
- Microservices
- Advanced analytics
- Loyalty/referral programs
